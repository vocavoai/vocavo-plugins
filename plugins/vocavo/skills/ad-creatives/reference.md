# Ad creatives: reference

## Formats per placement

| Placement | Ratio | Pixels | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Facebook and Instagram feed | 4:5 (preferred), 1:1 | 1080×1350, 1080×1080 | 4:5 takes the most screen on a phone |
| Stories | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | top and bottom 14 % (about 250 px) stay free; one 9:16 version for stories and reels keeps the reels limit below |
| Reels | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | top 14 % and bottom 35 % (about 670 px) stay free: name, caption, buttons |
| Right column, search | 1:1 | 1080×1080 | usually left to automatic placements |

Video: MP4 or MOV, 15–45 s for cuts, captions burned in, the first frame already shows the hook. Keep files small enough to upload (a few hundred MB at most).

## Phone legibility

- On a 1080 px wide canvas, text at least about 48 px high; headlines larger. Five to seven words on an image at most.
- Contrast: dark text on light or light on dark, never text over a busy photo without a solid band.
- Check every creative at the size a phone shows it (about a third of the canvas) before the preview.

## Cropping checklist

- Faces whole, with room above the head; no cut chin, forehead or hands mid-gesture.
- No text, logo or badge touching an edge or a safe zone.
- Product, screenshot or person is the largest element; nothing covers it.

## Claims and Meta's policies (summary, not legal advice)

- **Personal attributes:** no text that asserts or implies the viewer's financial status, health, religion, ethnicity, sexual orientation, criminal record or similar ("Du hast Schulden?", "Als Mensch mit …"). Talk about the topic, not about the viewer.
- **Business opportunities and earnings:** promises of quick or high income, "passives Einkommen", before/after income are restricted or rejected. Results are stated only as the owner proves them, as typical or not, in their wording.
- **Before/after and sensational content**, misleading buttons (fake play buttons, fake system messages) are rejected.
- **Special ad categories** (credit, employment, housing, social or political issues) must be declared in the campaign; then targeting is restricted. Ask when the offer touches credit or jobs.
- German law (UWG) also forbids misleading claims and invented scarcity.
